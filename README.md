# splunk-customer-stories
This is doc I wrote for Splunk (now owned by Cisco). The stories were part of a book used to help customers understand the value of Splunk. They were designed to appeal to C-level execs, business leaders, engineers, and general audiences. 

To find compelling stories, we created a fun contest to encourage sales engineers to contribute 1-paragraph ideas to a SalesForce location. I then picked the most promising stories, interviewed the sales engineers, and replicated the ways the customer had used Splunk by using Splunk's "sand box" where I could work with sample data. I then wrote drafts, disguised to protect customer privacy, and distributed them to management, marketing, and sales for first approval. I told the stories using Splunk's fun, engaging voice, which you can see in some of the headings in the attached stories. After approval, the stories  went to Splunk's editing team and were then published online. 

Impact:  The stories were distributed both online from splunk.com, and directly by sales people to leads. Sales was very happy with my work; several sales people credited my stories with helping them to close multi-million dollar deals.
